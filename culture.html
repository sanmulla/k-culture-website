Place · Palace
Built: 1395

Gyeongbokgung Palace

Built in 1395 during the Joseon dynasty — Geunjeongjeon throne hall is a highlight. Visit for the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony and the National Folk Museum.

Architecture & Meaning

Symmetry, colors, and Confucian spatial order emphasize royal authority and harmony.

Visitor Tips

Wear comfortable shoes; consider a guided tour for the Secret Garden insights.

Tradition · Music
Pansori · Samulnori

Gugak (국악)

Essence: Traditional Korean music covering both court (jeongak) and folk forms. Includes pansori (narrative vocal art), sanjo (instrumental solo), and samulnori (percussion ensemble).

Instruments

Gayageum (zither), geomungo, daegeum (bamboo flute), janggu (hourglass drum), buk (barrel drum), piri (reed instrument).

Preservation & Today

Taught in conservatories, reinterpreted by modern composers, and performed at national festivals.

Quick Facts

Genres: Pansori, Sanjo, Jeongak, Minyo (folk songs)

Tradition · Dance
